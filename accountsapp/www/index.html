Gada Electronics

The onboarding app

A small shop for Gada Electronics. It sells electronics, and every order is posted to a double-entry ledger.

Open the shop Browse the catalog

What is in the app

Vouchers, a ledger and three reports

Sales and purchase invoices

Create them, submit them, cancel them. Each one writes a balanced pair of ledger entries.

Journal entries

Move value between accounts. The entry has to balance or it will not save.

Payments

Record what a customer paid. The invoice is marked paid and the ledger is updated.

Reports

Chart of accounts, profit and loss, and the balance sheet. All three read from the same ledger.

How an order works

What happens when you order

  1. Add items to the cart. Prices and stock are read again on the server when you check out.
  2. Submit the order. A sales invoice is created and submitted. The customer is debited, sales are credited, and stock comes off the shelf.
  3. Look at the ledger. Open the balance sheet or the profit and loss report. The order is already in there.
